Home Away From Home

Infants Day Care

A secure, nurturing and loving daycare program designed around your baby's individual patterns and development milestones.

Tender Loving Care

Our Infants Program

Our infants program is designed to offer your baby a home away from home. Your baby will thrive in an atmosphere of tender loving care and comfort offered from experienced, caring teachers.

Throughout the day, babies are given individual attention, cuddled, hugged, rocked, walked, played with and talked to. Music is a vital part of the Infant Room and will be heard constantly, filling their days with joy and auditory stimulation.

Daily Routines

Morning Arrival & Pick Up Reports

Morning Routine (Arrival)

In the morning of arrival time the parent will give the teacher a verbal report of any changes in the infant's routine or schedule, any special needs they may have for the day or problems encountered during the night that may have a negative influence on the baby's day.

Afternoon Routine (Pick Up)

Parents will be given a written report of the Infant's Daily Activities, Intake and Output, and events that may have occurred during the course of the day when they pick their child up in the afternoon.

Sensory Play

Baby Sensory Garden

Tap anywhere to create magical sparkles ✨ and pop the floating bubbles! A calming sensory experience for your little one.

🌸 Magic Touch + Bubble Pop

Watch colorful bubbles drift gently across the garden. Tap them to pop with a burst of sparkles! Touch any empty area to create magical star trails that follow your finger.

This calming sensory activity is designed for infants to develop hand-eye coordination, visual tracking, and cause-and-effect understanding.

Our Curriculum

Development & Milestones

Nurturing little minds through play, learning and real-world experiences.

Milestone 01

Tummy Time

Guided in our loving arms, your little one gently encourages their warm backs during floor playtime to build neck and core strength. We laugh, cheer, and clap at every little push-up.

Milestone 02

Sign & Speech

With soft whispers and warm smiles, our teachers guide your infant through baby signs and gentle verbal mimics, helping them communicate their needs before they can speak.

Milestone 03

Visual Wonders

We open their eyes to the beautiful world. Holding colorful wooden toys and pointing to bright picture books, teachers stimulate your baby's natural wonder and curiosity.

Milestone 04

Motor Journeys

Tenderly guided by reassuring hands, babies grasp soft textures, balance stacking cups, and crawl through safe play gyms, building the confidence to explore their surroundings.

Safety, Security & Cleanliness

Stringent standards exceeding Government & Local guidelines

Space & Laundering

Separate and appropriate designated areas are provided for sleeping, eating, and diapering.

Each infant has their own designated crib with sheets laundered weekly.

Exceeds Local, State, and Indian Government Guidelines.

Safe Sleep Practices

Strict compliance with international & national safe sleep recommendations.

No crib bumper pads, no loose blankets, and no toys inside sleep cribs.

Infants are put to sleep alone and always placed on their backs in their assigned space.
